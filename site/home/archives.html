An archive you
can explore.

Find a passage, follow a theme, return to the recording. Analysis becomes useful when you can see where an answer came from.

  1. Transcribe & timecode

    Connect the words to the recording, down to the moment they were spoken.

  2. Index & catalogue

    Organise subjects, works and references so the collection can be navigated.

  3. Retrieve & cite

    Bring back relevant passages with their sources attached.

  4. Review & connect

    The archive’s keeper reviews what becomes part of its shared knowledge.
