Proposed collaboration model

Archival Alliance.

An archive holder, a creative lab and distribution partners can come together around one production.

One production SPV per project

A special purpose vehicle brings the partners together for a specific work. The archive holder keeps ownership of the source material and licenses what the production needs. The partners agree how to share the new work.

Preserve what
makes it original.

Restoration, interpretation and newly generated work serve different purposes. Each starts with understanding the material and its limits.

Assess the collection

Review the condition, formats, available context and intended uses before choosing a treatment.

Restore with care

Restore damaged photographs, stabilise or upscale footage, and assess colourisation against period references. Keep the original available for comparison.

Keep the trail

Carry source references and treatment notes into the working collection and delivery.
